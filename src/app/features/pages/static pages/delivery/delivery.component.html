Shipments and Returns

Your Pack Shipment

Orders are generally dispatched within 1-2 business days after receipt of payment and are shipped via trusted courier services with tracking and drop-off without signature. We will provide you with a link to track your package online.

Shipping fees include handling and packing fees as well as postage costs. Handling fees are fixed, whereas transport fees vary according to total weight of the shipment. We advise you to group your items in one order. We cannot group two distinct orders placed separately, and shipping fees will apply to each of them.

Returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery. Items must be returned unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied by a receipt or proof of purchase. To initiate a return, please contact our support team with your order number and reason for return.
